# Citing

If you use `pizzetti` in your research, please cite:

- the accompanying paper: *A Fast Elementary Series to Calculate Limb-darkened Transit Flux*
  (in preparation);
- Mandel, K. & Agol, E. 2002, ApJL, 580, L171, for the exact solution used near the limb;
- Agol, E., Luger, R. & Foreman-Mackey, D. 2020, AJ, 159, 123, for the closed-form elliptic
  arguments.

`pizzetti` follows the interface of batman (Kreidberg, L. 2015, PASP, 127, 1161). Its exact limb
solver is adapted from the Pandora exomoon code (Hippke, M. & Heller, R. 2022, A&A, 662, A37).

The name honours Paolo Pizzetti (1860–1918), whose mean-value formula (Rend. Lincei 18, 182,
1909) is the mathematical basis of the series.
